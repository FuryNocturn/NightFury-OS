NightFury OS - prueba en QEMU / QEMU test
==========================================

ESPAÑOL
Contenido: BOOTX64.EFI (el sistema), arrancar.ps1 (lanza QEMU), LICENSE y NOTICE.
Requisitos: Windows y QEMU (https://www.qemu.org/download/#windows).
Arrancar:   powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File arrancar.ps1
Ctrl+Alt+G suelta el ratón de la ventana de QEMU.
Es una versión de prueba: no está probada en un PC real. Aquí no toca ningún disco tuyo
(usa disco.img, un fichero que se crea junto al guion).

ENGLISH
Contents: BOOTX64.EFI (the system), arrancar.ps1 (starts QEMU), LICENSE and NOTICE.
Requirements: Windows and QEMU (https://www.qemu.org/download/#windows).
Start:        powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File arrancar.ps1
Ctrl+Alt+G releases the mouse from the QEMU window.
This is a test version: it has not been tested on a real PC. Here it touches none of your
disks (it uses disco.img, a file created next to the script).
